How to play

  1. Clear lines to attack. Two or more lines at once sends garbage to your rival. Quads and T-spins send the most.
  2. Blow up bombs to dig out. Garbage rows carry a bomb. Land any piece on it and that row explodes.
  3. Top out, give a K.O. If your stack reaches the top, the rival whose garbage hit you scores a K.O. and you keep playing. Every top-out also counts against you in the standings. In matches with no time limit, getting K.O.'d as many times as the K.O. cap knocks you out.
  4. Win the clock. After 2:00 the most K.O.s wins. Ties go to lines sent.
  5. Power-ups (Power-up Battle, and rooms that switch them on). Clear the line holding a glowing orb to collect one, then press E to use it. Shield, Clean Up, Bomb Piece and Power Surge help you; Ink Splat, Fog, Hold Lock and Speed Up hit your target. Never in ranked play.
  6. Sudden death. Matches with no time limit (like Last One Standing) speed up after 3:00: pieces fall faster and attacks hit 1.5× harder, then 2× after 4:30.
  7. Classic is endless. No rivals, no garbage. The speed rises every 10 lines; play until you top out and chase your high score.
