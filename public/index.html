How to play

  1. Clear lines to attack. Two or more lines at once sends garbage to your rival. Quads and T-spins send the most.
  2. Blow up bombs to dig out. Garbage rows carry a bomb. Land any piece on it and that row explodes.
  3. Top out, give a K.O. If your stack reaches the top, your rival scores a K.O. and you keep playing.
  4. Win the clock. After 2:00 the most K.O.s wins. Ties go to lines sent.
  5. Classic is endless. No rivals, no garbage. The speed rises every 10 lines; play until you top out and chase your high score.
