Block Arena privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, your name and your profile picture. We don't ask for your email, contacts or anything else. You can remove Block Arena's access at any time in your Google Account (Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services).

If you signed in with Facebook, we receive your app-scoped Facebook ID, your name and your profile picture. We don't receive your email, posts or anything else, and we never post to Facebook for you. If you allow "App friends", Facebook tells us which of your friends also play Block Arena so we can add them as friends in the game; we don't see your other friends.

We store your rank points, ranked match results and game progress (level, best scores) so your rank follows you across devices. Your name, picture and rank appear on the public leaderboard.

A sign-in cookie keeps you logged in. Settings and progress for guests stay in your own browser.

Your in-game friends (people you added with a friend code, an add link or from an online room) can see when you're online, whether you're in a room or a match, and your room code so they can join you. You can remove a friend at any time.

When you share a rank, match result or achievement, we store the picture card you share so Facebook can show it. Anyone with the link can see that card.

When you play online, your game board and nickname are shared live with the other players in your room. Rooms aren't recorded.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time: open Settings → Account → Delete my account in the game. Everything tied to your account is removed immediately.

You can also remove Block Arena from your Facebook settings (Settings → Apps and websites) and choose to delete your data; Facebook then tells us and we delete your account automatically.

Contact

Questions about your data: contact the game's owner through the Facebook page or site where you found Block Arena.
